ONE-SHOT PLANNING WORKSHEET
By the Imgmov team, with AI assistance

PURPOSE — What should the viewer notice?


STARTING FRAME — Subject, position, setting and available reference:


ONE ACTION — What changes? Move extra events to later shots:


CAMERA — Fixed or one named move; what stays in frame?


TIMING AND FINAL FRAME — Duration, final visible state and any end hold:


CONSTRAINTS — What must stay unchanged? What may change?


UNCERTAINTY OR TRADE-OFF — Missing information and the choice you are making:


NEXT CHECK AND ONE REVISION — What will you inspect in a later draft?


REVISED SHOT BRIEF — Combine your decisions into one coherent description:
